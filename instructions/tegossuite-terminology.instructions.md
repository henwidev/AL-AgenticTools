# German tegossuite terminology

Use the following translations consistently in German prose. Adapt capitalization,
grammatical endings, and plurals to the sentence. Prefer specific compound terms
over translations of their individual words.

Keep technical identifiers and quoted UI labels unchanged when referring to their
exact spelling; provide the German explanation separately where helpful.
